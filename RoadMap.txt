🗺️ Revised Roadmap Based on Your Feedback
1. Job Visibility & Duration ✅
Revised Approach:

Jobs remain visible to hired worker until finalization (not just 24h)

Perfect for jobs scheduled days/weeks later

Worker can reference job details until completion

2. Contact Privacy ✅
Agreed: No contact details in app - chat only for communication

Maintains privacy and security

Users can share contact details via chat if they choose

No phone/email exposure in profiles

3. Location Granularity ✅
Implementation:

Enhance current location detection to get suburb + city

Store format: "City, Suburb" (e.g., "Johannesburg, Sandton")

Display full location in job details

Enable search by suburb within city

4. Maps & Navigation ✅
Google Maps Deep Links (No API needed):

https://maps.google.com/?q=FULL_ADDRESS - opens in user's Google Maps app

Perfect for turn-by-turn navigation

No cost, no API keys, works on all devices

5. Rating System ✅
Simplified: 1-5 stars only (no comments for now)

Clean and simple

Reduces complexity

Still provides valuable feedback

6. Financials ✅
Agreed: No payment integration

Pure connection platform only

Financial arrangements happen offline

Reduces liability and complexity

7. Terms & Conditions ✅
CRITICAL ADDITION:

Add T&C screen during signup

Cover liability, user conduct, privacy

Require acceptance before using app

Legal protection for platform

8. Job Status Management ✅
Enhanced Status Flow:

active → Open for applications

filled → Hired worker, no new applications

completed → Job done, rated

cancelled → Job cancelled (with notifications)

Cancellation Handling:

Notify hired worker immediately if job cancelled

Prevent "no show" frustrations

Cancel before hiring: simple removal

Cancel after hiring: require notification

9. Job Editing ✅
Essential Feature:

Edit button on employer's job posts

Allow changes to date, time, details

Notify applicants of significant changes

Prevent complete re-posting for minor edits
